The big idea

Say a game.
Play it two minutes later.

“Make a game where the floor is lava and you have to stand on one leg.” The TV writes it, tests it, and the whole room plays it.

This is the heart of it. A kid describes a game out loud. A coding agent writes it, an automatic play-test checks it works, and it appears on the TV for everyone in the room. Typically one and a half to three minutes.
