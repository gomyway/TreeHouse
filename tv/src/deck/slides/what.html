What it is

A party room on the AI TV, hosted by the TV itself.

1

Scan to join

Phones in the room scan a QR code on the TV. No app, no account.

2

Talk to play

The TV’s voice agent hosts: reads the code, starts games, cracks jokes.

3

Phones or bodies

Answer on your phone, or play with your body in front of the TV camera.

Everything happens on one shared screen in the living room. Phones are just controllers: they show whatever the TV sends, so a new game never needs a phone update. The TV’s own agent is the host, by voice.
