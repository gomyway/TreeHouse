Game 1 · AI literacy, as a party game

Who’s the Bot?

Everyone answers a silly prompt on their phone. One or two AI players secretly answer too, written to sound human. The room votes the funniest and hunts the bot. The final round flips it: find the one human among the bots.

The AI players are tuned to blend in: they match the room’s style, add the room’s own typos, and answer on a human clock. Kids get better at spotting AI writing, which is a real skill now, and it’s just a party game.
