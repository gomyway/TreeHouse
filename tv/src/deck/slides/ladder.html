For young creators

A ladder from player to creator, climbed while having fun.

STEP 1

Play

Party games with family and friends.

STEP 2

Tweak

“Make the bots sneakier.” “Make it about dinosaurs.”

STEP 3

Invent

Describe a brand-new game out loud.

STEP 4 · NEXT

Peek inside

See the rules and code the agent wrote, then change them.

Interest curation (next): the TV remembers which games a kid invents and loves, and suggests the next creative step, like adding a new rule or a camera move.

Steps 1 to 3 work today: play, steer games by voice, and invent new ones. Step 4 and interest curation are next: showing kids what the agent built, in plain words and real code, and nudging each kid toward the next small creative step based on what they enjoy. That’s how a kid gets curious about AI coding without a lesson.
