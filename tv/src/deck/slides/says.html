Game 2 · off the couch

Little Says

Simon Says, called by the TV’s voice and judged by its camera. Arms up, airplane, hands on head, lean, squat, jump, combos, and “just kidding!” traps. No phone needed.

For parents: screen time that gets everyone moving and laughing together.

The TV sees skeletons, never video.

The TV camera tracks up to two players as skeleton points only, no pictures. We wrote our own move detection from those points today: arm raises, airplane arms, hands on head, leaning, squats and jumps, so the game gets harder level by level.
