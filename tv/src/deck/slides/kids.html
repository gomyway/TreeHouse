Who it’s for · the kids

Today’s kids don’t just play games. They want to make them.

Born builders

On game-building platforms, kids already design worlds and rules for each other.

The wall

Turning an idea into a working game still means learning a whole editor and a language.

Alone on a screen

Most of that creating happens solo, on a personal device, away from the family.

The audience: kids roughly the age of today’s game-building platform users. They are already creators. What stops them is the gap between an idea and a working game, and most of it happens alone on a personal screen.
