Treehouse

The party TV where kids make games, not just play them.

Leo & Nelson · built today with Claude

Treehouse is a party room for the Little AI TV. Today we built two games, a camera that judges body moves, and something bigger: you describe a game out loud and the TV builds it in a couple of minutes. This deck is about who that is for: young creators, and the parents who worry about them.
